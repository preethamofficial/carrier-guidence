TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Understand how recommendations are made.

CodeAtlas helps you explore a direction. It does not predict hiring outcomes, promise salaries or claim that one career is objectively best for every graduate.

How the career assessment works

  1. You answer 30 questions about interests, preferred work, coding comfort, analysis, creativity, communication, systems and priorities.
  2. Each option contributes weighted points to work dimensions. Each role has a profile of those dimensions.
  3. The assessment normalizes each role's selected points against that role's possible weighted score, then sorts the results. The displayed percentage is a relative match for your answers—not a probability of getting a job or a validated psychometric score.
  4. Use the top three as hypotheses to test. Read role roadmaps, build a small project, talk to people in the field and compare current job descriptions before committing.

Role preparation timelines are broad learning-planning estimates. They depend on prior knowledge, time, practice quality, local market requirements and portfolio evidence. They are not guarantees.

Job feed and data quality

The Recent Jobs page reads configured public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job-board endpoints through a daily GitHub Actions collector. It stores the original application URL, source date when available, first-seen date, and a best-effort refresh timestamp.

  • Unknown posting dates are not presented as verified recent.
  • A source timestamp may be an update time rather than the original publication time.
  • Open the original employer posting and verify the vacancy before applying.
  • CodeAtlas currently avoids publishing unsourced salary figures or live hiring-demand statistics as facts.
